# 🎬 Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard

An interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard analyzing Netflix's 
global streaming content library with rich visuals and filters.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

1. Title Page
- Project intro: "An Interactive Analysis of Global Streaming Content"
- Created by Harish

2. Overview
- Total Titles: 5,336
- Total Movies: 5,188
- Total TV Shows: 147
- Total Genres: 34
- Movies vs TV Shows comparison (donut chart)
- Top genres: Dramas (1,518), Comedies (1,127), Action & Adventure (806)
- Global content map by country

3. Content Trends
- Titles added by year (peak around 2019-2020)
- Titles released per year trend
- Genre popularity over years
- Movies vs TV Shows growth over time
- Release year filter (1960–2021)

4. Genres and Ratings
- Most common rating: TV-MA (34.15%)
- Top genres: Dramas, Comedies, Action & Adventure
- Title count by rating and type (Movie vs TV Show)
- Treemap of genres

5. Genre Details
- Detailed table: Title, Type, Rating, Country, Release Year, Duration
- Searchable and filterable content list

6. Geography and Production
- USA leads with 2,133 titles (2,102 Movies + 31 TV Shows)
- India ranks 2nd with 910 titles
- UK 3rd with 347 titles
- Global map visualization by country

7. Mobile View
- Title Count: 5,336
- Movies Count: 5,188
- TV Shows Count: 147

8. Duration and Format
- Average Movie Duration: 102.43 minutes
- Max Seasons (TV Show): 15
- Most movies are 80–100 minutes long
- Most TV shows have only 1 season

9. Content Explorer
- Full searchable table by country, genre, rating
- Filter by country and genre
- Movie duration heatmap

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)
- Netflix excel Dataset

## 💡 Key Insights
- Netflix has 97.24% Movies vs 2.76% TV Shows
- Dramas is the most popular genre (1,518 titles)
- USA is the top content producer (2,133 titles)
- India ranks 2nd globally with 910 titles
- TV-MA is the most common content rating (34.15%)
- Average movie duration is 102 minutes
- Netflix content additions peaked around 2019–2020
